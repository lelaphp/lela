description: Grill one decision ticket with the design of record answering in place of a person. `/grill-design <n>`; with no number, the branch's ticket.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

A decision ticket is a question a build ticket waits on, and on a track nobody sits at the table, so the design of record answers the grilling: `third-engine-design.md`, `jit-design.md`, `harness-design.md`, the ADRs under `docs/adr/` and php-src at the tag `oracle.toml` pins. What they say is recorded on the ticket, cited, and closes it; what they leave unsaid is the edge of autonomy and goes to a person. The skill decides nothing the design is silent on: a resolution is a passage of the sources with its citation, the session's own preference lives in the recommendation beside a question and nowhere else, and an ADR or a design revision is a person's (harness-design.md §11), so the session proposes the sentence and never writes it into the design.

**Putting a question to the design.** The sources are asked in order, and the first that decides the question answers it: the three design documents, a ledger entry counting as the section it revised; then `docs/adr/*.md`, an ADR outranking the section it reversed; then php-src at the pin, the tree at `TEST_PHP_SRCDIR` when set and `oracle.toml`'s `[source].path` otherwise, which answers a question about behaviour php shows, since AGENTS.md's second principle says copy php's algorithm where it is observable, and nothing about this engine's own structure. A source *answers* when one passage decides the question as asked, with no gap the session's judgement would fill, and the citation names the passage: `third-engine-design.md §5.6`, `harness-design.md §4.5 item 7`, `ADR-0006`, `Zend/zend_vm_def.h:1234 at php-8.5.9`. A source is *silent* when no passage decides it, when the design marks the point open itself (harness-design.md §13, `(unverified)` inline) or when two sources disagree, since which is fixed is a person's call. The recommendation is never the answer: where the passage and the recommendation differ, the passage is what is recorded. Finding facts is a subagent's work with a bounded sweep, the sections it may read, a cap of a few files in full and the question it settles; the track's map, the `frontier:map` issue when the track has one, bounds the tree with its destination, and its decisions-so-far point at passages other sessions found.

**The rounds.** `gh issue view <n>`: the ticket's question is the root of the tree, its acceptance boxes say what a resolution must cover, and its `blocking:` list names the build tickets waiting on it. Work the tree in rounds as `/grilling` does: the frontier is every question whose prerequisites are settled; ask the whole frontier in one round, numbered, each with a recommended answer, then put each to the sources; a question that depends on one still open this round waits for the next. Write each question up as it settles, `Q3 - <title>: <question>`, `Recommended: <what the session would answer>`, `Answer: <the passage, quoted or tightly paraphrased> (<citation>)`, or `Silent: <what was looked for, in which sections>` in place of the answer. Recompute the frontier and go on; the rounds end when it is empty, or when a question is silent and the rest of the frontier not downstream of it is settled.

**The design answered every question.** Post the resolution as a comment on the ticket, `**Decision.** <the resolution in a paragraph, each clause with its citation>` and then the questions in tree order as written up; this comment is the record a later session reads and the map gists. Tick every acceptance box the resolution meets, `gh issue edit <n> --body-file`, the box text unchanged; a box the resolution does not meet is a question the tree missed, so go back to the rounds. Then `cargo xtask ticket read <n>` and `cargo xtask ticket close <n> --opens-nothing`, or `--opens <m>` for each ticket the grilling filed. Nothing is committed: the record is the ticket, and the close names the commit it was read at.

**A question is silent.** Post a comment on the decision ticket with the tree so far and the silent question marked, so the next session starts where this one stopped. File one ticket per silent question, `gh issue create --label for-a-person`, carrying the decision ticket's `track:` label when it has one, in the house shape: `## What happened`, the silent question, the tree above it and where the sources were looked, by section; `## What to build`, the sentence the design needs and the section or ADR it belongs in, a revision being a person's act; `## Acceptance criteria`, a box that the section or ADR says it and a box that the decision ticket, #<n>, can be answered from it; `## Blocked by`, none. Wire it, `gh issue edit <person> --add-blocking <n>,<build>,...`, onto the decision ticket and every build ticket its `blocking:` list names, so each shows the person's ticket under `blocked-by` and none sits in the frontier `track next` reads. End with the decision ticket open: the Stop hook holds the turn once for its unticked boxes and the second Stop passes, and `track work` leaves the worktree standing for a person. That is the ending, not a defect to route around.
