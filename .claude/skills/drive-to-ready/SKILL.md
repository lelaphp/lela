# Drive to ready

You have the **mandate**, whole: every `ready-for-agent` ticket is yours to group, order, absorb, rewrite, reprioritize, build, or close as already met, duplicated or superseded, and what lands on `main` is yours to answer for. The **backlog** is every open `ready-for-agent` ticket. The order of work is your own list of it, `BACKLOG.md`, and where the run stands is the checkpoint, `ROADMAP.md`; both are files of yours, and no issue on the tracker keeps either. For a ticket that waits on a person, you keep its blocker filed and precise.

The loop's mechanics belong to /specs: slots, seats, cuts, collisions, the merge check, the three phases. Read `.claude/skills/specs/SKILL.md` at the start and again after any compaction. This file decides *what* the loop works on; /specs decides *how*. /specs, /build, /frontier and /grill-design are user-invoked, so you and your builders read them by path under `.claude/skills/`.

## Stance

**Scope is the backlog**: every free slot takes its next unblocked ticket. **Do it now**: work found mid-ticket is done in this run, in that ticket when it is on its path and within its size, otherwise as a ticket you dispatch next; work bigger than a slot's size is cut into tickets and queued in the backlog order. The only ticket you file is a `for-a-person` one, for an act harness §11 gives a person. **Critic**: read the design of record for an area before cutting there. When a refactor or rewrite is the right shape, it is the ticket: priced with /rust-invoice, reasoned under What to build, and bounded to one path. **Push back** with evidence, through /grill-design, when a ticket's premise contradicts the code or the design; never build to it.

## The loop

1. **Own the checkpoint.** Open `~/Projects/dax-php-worktrees/_coordinator/ROADMAP.md`. If its `Owner` names another session that is still running, stop and report that; there is one coordinator at a time. Otherwise rewrite it to the shape under *The checkpoint*. If it isn't in that shape yet, first move it aside as `ROADMAP.before-<date>.md`, as earlier roadmaps were. Reconcile the rewrite with `git worktree list`, `gh issue list --label ready-for-agent`, and the runtime's live tasks. An agent you don't remember may still be running: confirm whether it is before replacing its work. Done when every slot line matches a live task or a branch.
2. **Read the backlog.** Familiarize yourself with every open `ready-for-agent` ticket and its dependencies: its body (`gh issue list --label ready-for-agent --state open --json number,title,labels,body`), what blocks it and what it blocks (`gh api repos/{owner}/{repo}/issues/<n>/dependencies/blocked_by`, and `/blocking`), and its code path. Check each against `main`: one already met, duplicated or superseded you close now with `cargo xtask ticket close <n>` (`--withdrawn` with the measurement, where nothing was built). Keep one line per ticket in `~/Projects/dax-php-worktrees/_coordinator/BACKLOG.md`, the unblocked ones in dependency order, those that unblock the most first. Done when every open `ready-for-agent` ticket has its line there.
3. **Choose.** Fill each free slot in this order: a regression or a blocker on a merge; then the backlog's next unblocked ticket in BACKLOG.md's order. Cut by **cohesion**, one code path per group, as /specs §1–3 cut, bundling backlog tickets that share a path into one ticket. A decision ticket goes to /grill-design, an empty frontier to /frontier. Done when each free slot has a ticket number and its path, and no two slots share a path.
4. **Dispatch** one brief per builder, as *Briefs* says. Done when each brief names its ticket, checkout, branch, owned paths, acceptance boxes and model.
5. **Yield.** Completions arrive through the runtime; don't poll or sleep. When work returns, run /specs' integrating and publishing phases over what is ready, and dispatch the work each handoff names as found and not done. When priorities change, move capacity at the next safe checkpoint (a builder's command finished or parked), never mid-command.
6. **Checkpoint.** At every dispatch, ready, merge, publish or change of priority, rewrite the sections that changed. After every publication, update the BACKLOG.md lines it closed or unblocked and return to step 2.

## The checkpoint

`ROADMAP.md` is an **index**, not a log: a fresh coordinator or a person reads it cold in one pass. Every section is rewritten in place, it stays under 80 lines, and it links to git, the tracker, the store and the runtime rather than copying them. Command logs, per-command timestamps, seat lines, finished handles and anything `git log`, `gh` or `xtask status` can answer stay out. Refer to tickets by title with the number inside, "Every run works in its own pinned tree", and set short hashes apart from the words around them. Its sections, in order: `Owner: <session>, since <UTC>`; **Backlog**, the open `ready-for-agent` count, how many are unblocked, and the next three in BACKLOG.md's order; **Phase**, building, integrating or publishing, with base, candidate and the comparisons still owed; **Slots**, a table of ticket, checkout and branch, model, and state since UTC; **Ready, not merged**, each with its branch and head; **Waiting on a person**, each with its exact condition; **Next**, what fills the next free slot and why, in a line or two.

## Briefs

A subagent knows only its brief. The team is flat: you dispatch every builder, a builder may dispatch the reviews its build needs, and reviewers and advisors dispatch no one. Use a temporary lead only for a bounded objective of several coordinated subtasks, with a share of the three slots, a deliverable and an end condition. Models: a builder is Opus at medium for a well-specified ticket on one path, Opus at high for interacting engine invariants, and Haiku at high for mechanical work (a lock line, a re-pin, a rename across call sites). An advisor is Opus at high, for one question whose answer changes the work. A reviewer is Haiku at high for compliance with the ticket, AGENTS.md and the glossary, and Fable at high for unsafe, GC, frame transitions and concurrency. Escalate the model when the reasoning is hard; when evidence, a permission or the boundary is missing, fix the brief instead.
- **Builder:** the ticket title (#n). Its checkout's absolute path, branch `t<n>` and base hash, where it runs /build's preflight first. The paths it owns and the paths other slots own. Done means the acceptance boxes plus a ready record per `.claude/skills/build/SKILL.md`. Build as /build says, with /tdd at the witness. Absorb a gap on its path that fits the ticket; name any other found work in the handoff, with its path, for you to dispatch, and file only a person's act. The handoff gives ticket, head and base, ready record or blocker, found work, command exits, and resources released.
- **Advisor:** one question and the decision its answer changes. The design sections, php-src files at the pinned tag, and readings to read. A recommendation with each claim cited; the advisor edits nothing.
- **Reviewer:** the diff base..head. The standard: the ticket's boxes, AGENTS.md and CONTEXT.md's _Avoid_ lists, plus the invariants in question for a Fable review. Findings each with file:line and a concrete failure scenario. "Nothing found" is an answer.

## Report and finish

Tell the user only when a batch publishes or a person is needed: one line each, with what moved and the evidence. The mission is done when every open `ready-for-agent` ticket is landed or blocked by a filed `for-a-person` ticket that names its exact condition. An empty frontier proves nothing, and waiting on builders or CI is a yield.
