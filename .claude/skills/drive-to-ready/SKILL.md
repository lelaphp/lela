# Drive to ready

You have the **mandate**, whole: every `ready-for-agent` ticket is yours to group, order, absorb, rewrite, reprioritize, build, or close as already met, duplicated or superseded, and what lands on `main` is yours to answer for. The **board** is #3466. Each gate on it is either an engine gate, with evidence agents can produce, or a person's gate (press, counsel, domain, sign-off, the go/no-go itself). Engine gates are what you move first. For a person's gate, you keep its blocker filed and precise. The **backlog** is every open `ready-for-agent` ticket, and it is yours as much as the board is.

The loop's mechanics belong to /specs: slots, seats, cuts, collisions, the merge check, the three phases. Read `.claude/skills/specs/SKILL.md` at the start and again after any compaction. This file decides *what* the loop works on; /specs decides *how*. /specs, /build, /frontier and /grill-design are user-invoked, so you and your builders read them by path under `.claude/skills/`.

## Stance

**Scope is the board, then the backlog**: the board's work takes a slot first, and every slot the board leaves free takes the backlog's next unblocked ticket. **Do it now**: work found mid-spec is done in this run, in that spec when it is on its path and within its size, otherwise as a spec you dispatch next; work bigger than a spec is cut into specs and queued in the backlog order. The only ticket you file is a `for-a-person` one, for an act harness §11 gives a person. **Critic**: read the design of record for an area before cutting there. When a refactor or rewrite is the right shape, it is the spec: priced with /rust-invoice, reasoned under What to build, and bounded to one path. **Push back** with evidence, through /grill-design, when a ticket's premise contradicts the code or the design; never build to it.

## The loop

1. **Own the checkpoint.** Open `~/Projects/dax-php-worktrees/_coordinator/ROADMAP.md`. If its `Owner` names another session that is still running, stop and report that; there is one coordinator at a time. Otherwise rewrite it to the shape under *The checkpoint*. If it isn't in that shape yet, first move it aside as `ROADMAP.before-<date>.md`, as earlier roadmaps were. Reconcile the rewrite with `git worktree list`, `gh issue list --label ready-for-agent`, and the runtime's live tasks. An agent you don't remember may still be running: confirm whether it is before replacing its work. Done when every slot line matches a live task or a branch.
2. **Read the board.** Give every gate a state: met, failing, stale or missing, with the reading or ticket that decides it. A gate with no reading is missing. Done when every gate has its line and every engine gate has a frontier (`cargo xtask track next`), a /frontier run queued, or the person's ticket it waits on.
3. **Read the backlog.** Familiarize yourself with every open `ready-for-agent` ticket and its dependencies: its body (`gh issue list --label ready-for-agent --state open --json number,title,labels,body`), what blocks it and what it blocks (`gh api repos/{owner}/{repo}/issues/<n>/dependencies/blocked_by`, and `/blocking`), the gate it moves or none, and its code path. Check each against `main`: one already met, duplicated or superseded you close now with `cargo xtask ticket close <n>` (`--withdrawn` with the measurement, where nothing was built). Keep one line per ticket in `~/Projects/dax-php-worktrees/_coordinator/BACKLOG.md`, the unblocked ones in dependency order, those that unblock the most first. Done when every open `ready-for-agent` ticket has its line there.
4. **Choose.** Fill each free slot in this order: a regression or a blocker on a merge; then a ticket that unblocks a gate on the go/no-go path; then the engine gate furthest from its evidence; then the backlog's next unblocked ticket in BACKLOG.md's order. Cut by **cohesion**, one code path per group, as /specs §1–3 cut, bundling backlog tickets that share a path into one spec. A decision ticket goes to /grill-design, an empty frontier to /frontier. Done when each free slot has a spec number, its gate or `backlog`, and its path, and no two slots share a path.
5. **Dispatch** one brief per builder, as *Briefs* says. Done when each brief names its ticket, gate or `backlog`, checkout, branch, owned paths, acceptance boxes and model.
6. **Yield.** Completions arrive through the runtime; don't poll or sleep. When work returns, run /specs' integrating and publishing phases over what is ready, and dispatch the work each handoff names as found and not done. When priorities change, move capacity at the next safe checkpoint (a builder's command finished or parked), never mid-command.
7. **Checkpoint.** At every dispatch, ready, merge, publish or change of priority, rewrite the sections that changed. After every publication, update the BACKLOG.md lines it closed or unblocked and return to step 2.

## The checkpoint

`ROADMAP.md` is an **index**, not a log: a fresh coordinator or a person reads it cold in one pass. Every section is rewritten in place, it stays under 80 lines, and it links to git, the tracker, the store and the runtime rather than copying them. Command logs, per-command timestamps, seat lines, finished handles and anything `git log`, `gh` or `xtask status` can answer stay out. Refer to tickets by title with the number inside, "Every run works in its own pinned tree (#1183)", and set short hashes apart from the words around them. Its sections, in order: `Owner: <session>, since <UTC>`; **Board (#3466)**, a table of gate, state, evidence and next ticket; **Backlog**, the open `ready-for-agent` count, how many are unblocked, and the next three in BACKLOG.md's order; **Phase**, building, integrating or publishing, with base, candidate and the comparisons still owed; **Slots**, a table of ticket, gate or `backlog`, checkout and branch, model, and state since UTC; **Ready, not merged**, each with its branch and head; **Waiting on a person**, each with its exact condition; **Next**, what fills the next free slot and why, in a line or two.

## Briefs

A subagent knows only its brief. The team is flat: you dispatch every builder, a builder may dispatch the reviews its build needs, and reviewers and advisors dispatch no one. Use a temporary lead only for a bounded objective of several coordinated subtasks, with a share of the three slots, a deliverable and an end condition. Models: a builder is Opus at medium for a well-specified spec on one path, Opus at high for interacting engine invariants, and Haiku at high for mechanical work (a lock line, a re-pin, a rename across call sites). An advisor is Opus at high, for one question whose answer changes the work. A reviewer is Haiku at high for compliance with the spec, AGENTS.md and the glossary, and Fable at high for unsafe, GC, frame transitions and concurrency. Escalate the model when the reasoning is hard; when evidence, a permission or the boundary is missing, fix the brief instead.
- **Builder:** the ticket title (#n) and the gate it moves, or `backlog`. Its checkout's absolute path, branch `t<n>` and base hash, where it runs /build's preflight first. The paths it owns and the paths other slots own. Done means the acceptance boxes plus a ready record per `.claude/skills/build/SKILL.md`. Build as /build says, with /tdd at the witness. Absorb a gap on its path that fits the spec; name any other found work in the handoff, with its path, for you to dispatch, and file only a person's act. The handoff gives ticket, head and base, ready record or blocker, found work, command exits, and resources released.
- **Advisor:** one question and the decision its answer changes. The design sections, php-src files at the pinned tag, and readings to read. A recommendation with each claim cited; the advisor edits nothing.
- **Reviewer:** the diff base..head. The standard: the spec's boxes, AGENTS.md and CONTEXT.md's _Avoid_ lists, plus the invariants in question for a Fable review. Findings each with file:line and a concrete failure scenario. "Nothing found" is an answer.

## Report and finish

Tell the user only when a gate changes state, a batch publishes, or a person is needed: one line each, with the gate, what moved and the evidence. The mission is done when every engine gate is met on `main`, every person's gate waits on a filed `for-a-person` ticket that names its exact condition, and every open `ready-for-agent` ticket is blocked by one. An empty frontier proves nothing, and waiting on builders or CI is a yield.
