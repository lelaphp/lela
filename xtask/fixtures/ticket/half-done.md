## What to build

The negative control of a withdrawal (#185): a ticket one box of whose
criteria is ticked. Work half done is not withdrawn; it is finished, or
its box is unticked by a person first. `cargo xtask ticket close <n>
--withdrawn <reason>` over this body fails, names the ticked box and asks
the tracker nothing.
