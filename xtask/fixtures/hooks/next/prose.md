# Prose that names NEXT and cites no revision

A branch cites the revision it adds as `revision NEXT`, and its row as
`**NEXT. Revision NEXT (<date>).**`, until the merge numbers both.

The dump prints an append's operand as NEXT, as in INIT_ARRAY 3 CV0($a) NEXT.

Revision 43 stands; NEXT the merge numbers the row after it.

The revisions are numbered at the merge, and the NEXT one is 44.
