# A glossary of two sections

The shape of `CONTEXT.md`: a term is a `**Name**:` line under a heading,
its `_Avoid_` line the words the term replaces. A rule that names the
Harness section is held to the terms under that heading alone.

## Language

### Engine

**Serve**:
The one request transaction.
_Avoid_: run, execute

**Linked table**:
The frozen form of declarations after linking.
_Avoid_: registry

### Harness

**Tally**:
The one module behind the corpus lanes.
_Avoid_: scoreboard, summary

**Reading**:
The one-shaped value an instrument writes after a run.
_Avoid_: result (which means `Result` in Rust), report
