# A glossary of one section

The shape of `CONTEXT.md`: a term is a `**Name**:` line under a heading,
its `_Avoid_` line the words the term replaces. A host is held to every
section.

## Language

### Engine

**Serve**:
The one request transaction.
_Avoid_: run, execute

**Linked table**:
The frozen form of declarations after linking.
_Avoid_: registry

### Harness

**Reading**:
The one-shaped value an instrument writes after a run.
_Avoid_: report
